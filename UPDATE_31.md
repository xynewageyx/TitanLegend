# Titan Legends – Update 31

- Four new low-poly mob silhouette accents: horned beast, crystal crawler, armored guardian and elemental wisp.
- Colors of accents follow the 10 existing biome elements.
- Small emissive chest gem helps differentiate mobs.
- Integrated directly into `battle_manager.gd` for every non-boss mob spawn.
- Boss auras, portrait chat and combat systems from Update 30 remain unchanged.
- Lightweight meshes only; no external textures or particle systems.
- Runtime test in Godot/Android is still required.
