# Titan Legends — stylized armor and weapons art direction

All weapon and armor models MUST retain a colorful, rounded, exaggerated, friendly 3D cartoon mobile RPG appearance inspired by Ulala, without copying proprietary assets.

## Silhouette
- Chibi heroes: oversized head, short torso and limbs, readable silhouette.
- Large exaggerated weapons, rounded beveled edges, chunky armor plates.
- Higher rarity increases scale, layered details, fantasy spikes and floating crystals, but NEVER shifts to realistic horror or gritty photorealism.
- Avoid tiny details that disappear on 720x1280 portrait screens.

## Six rarities
0 Common: simple wood, leather, iron; compact.
1 Uncommon: reinforced shapes, green accents.
2 Rare: blue magical runes, thicker weapon heads, emissive accents.
3 Epic: gold trim, larger shoulders, decorative blunt cartoon spikes.
4 Legendary: orange accents, dragon-themed motifs, animated glowing core.
5 Mythic: purple accents, hovering crystal components, aura and particles.

## Armor
Class silhouettes: tank broad and round; warrior sturdy; assassin narrow and agile; mage robe and floating crystals; paladin polished gold and light; healer soft cloth and nature/light.
Slots: helmet, chest, shoulders, arms, gloves, legs, boots, back.
Each slot should remain recognizable across six tiers.

## Technical
- Godot 4.x GL Compatibility; Android-friendly meshes and material counts.
- glTF/GLB preferred for final rigged models.
- Keep all effects optional for low-end devices.
- Procedural preview scripts are prototypes, not production-ready skinned armor.
- Existing procedural scripts need mesh bevels, curves and toon materials before art approval.
