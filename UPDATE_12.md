# Titan Legends – Update 12: Klassen-Kampfeffekte

- Procedural, mobile-friendly attack projectiles and impact flashes for all 11 classes.
- Healers display green healing pulses every third attack tick.
- Tanks display blue defensive pulses every third attack tick.
- Effects use low-poly meshes and Tween cleanup; no external art assets.
- Passive pets remain passive; class selection and saved progress remain unchanged.
